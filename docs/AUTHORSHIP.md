# Who authored a program, and what rights it has

Programs here are CC0: anyone can use them for anything. If an AI wrote a program from your prompts, current US guidance says prompts alone don't make you its author, so much of it may not be copyrightable at all. What you choose, arrange and edit yourself is your expression. Under CC0 you give that away too. Every edit gets a new CID and lineage records which version came from which, so your choices are on the record. Want to keep something? Keep it private on your own devices: private work is sealed and never stored here.

Source: the US Copyright Office's page on [copyright and artificial intelligence](https://www.copyright.gov/ai/).
