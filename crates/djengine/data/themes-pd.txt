# BeatScript public-domain theme library v1: melodies, basses and figures of music first published before 1929, as BeatScript bars.
# This file is CC0 1.0 Universal. Every melody in it is in the public domain, composed and first published before 1929, and each bar is
# written from the melody itself (the notes the composer wrote, read as a person hums them), never from a modern arrangement, edition,
# transcription or recording. Curated by hand, not scraped; small on purpose. The melodic twin of phrasebook-pd.txt. Docs: docs/beatscript/forms.md.
# id | role | key | mode | meter | steps | bars | work | composer | year | pairs | note
#   id         what a program names: `let` names are the id (`ode_to_joy`, an alternation of its bars) and its bars (`ode_to_joy_1` ...)
#   role       one of: melody, bass, harmony, arp (the part it is written for)
#   key        the key the bars are written in (a BeatScript key word: `d`, `b`, `db`); transpose with `transpose(id, n)` or `~ id_* up n`
#   mode       one of: major, minor, modal (a pedal or an ostinato that is neither)
#   meter      the time signature as written (`4/4`, `2/4`, `3/4`, `5/4`); steps: the sixteenths a bar of the pattern takes on the grid
#              (16 for 4/4 and for two bars of 2/4, 12 for 3/4, 20 for 5/4): a part plays it as `melody`, `melody/12`, `bass/20`
#   bars       the bars, ` ; ` apart, each one bar of `steps` terms: notes (`f#4`), chords (`[b3, d4, f#4]`) and rests (`~`); a bar is let-ready
#   year       the year it was composed, or first published where that is the earlier date known (all before 1929); the note says which
#   pairs      themes that share its key or its harmony and morph well with it (`~ a_* morph b_1 0.25`), comma apart
